Word lists

Ready-made words and sentences for the Words page of the Studying Abroad app. Download a list, then add it in the app.

How to add a list

  1. Tap Download below and save the file on your phone.
  2. Open the app, go to Words and set the language (for example Chinese).
  3. Tap Add from file (at the bottom of the Words page), choose the file, then Add.

Words you already have are skipped and never changed. The app shows you a list of what was skipped.

Chinese (Mandarin)

Every list has characters, pinyin, English, and Thai in the note. Words go to Words in the app, sentences to Sentences. No list repeats another.
